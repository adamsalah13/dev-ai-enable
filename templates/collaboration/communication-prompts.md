# Communication AI Prompts

## Meeting Facilitation
### Pre-Meeting
- "Generate agenda for [meeting type] about [topic]"
- "Create pre-meeting questions for stakeholders about [project]"
- "Prepare decision framework for [decision topic]"

### During Meeting
- "Summarize key points from this discussion: [discussion notes]"
- "Identify action items from meeting notes: [notes]"
- "Generate follow-up questions for clarification on: [topic]"

### Post-Meeting
- "Create meeting summary with action items: [meeting notes]"
- "Generate follow-up email template for: [meeting outcome]"
- "Document decisions made and rationale: [decision notes]"

## Stakeholder Communication
### Status Updates
- "Create project status update for [stakeholder type]: [current status]"
- "Generate executive summary of [project milestone]"
- "Prepare risk communication for: [identified risks]"

### Issue Communication
- "Draft incident communication for: [incident description]"
- "Create user-friendly explanation of: [technical issue]"
- "Generate FAQ responses for: [common concerns]"

## Team Communication
### Daily Standups
- "Summarize yesterday's progress: [work completed]"
- "Identify blockers and dependencies: [current situation]"
- "Plan today's priorities: [available tasks]"

### Cross-Team Collaboration
- "Explain technical concept to non-technical stakeholders: [concept]"
- "Translate business requirements to technical terms: [requirements]"
- "Create shared understanding document for: [complex topic]"

## Documentation Communication
- "Convert technical specification to user guide: [spec]"
- "Create announcement for new feature: [feature description]"
- "Generate training materials for: [new process/tool]"
